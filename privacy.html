Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Neural Rewiring Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Neural Rewiring to upload that channel's own videos. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for two permissions on that one YouTube account:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's videos, Shorts and thumbnails.
  • Manage the account (youtube.force-ssl): to set a video's privacy status (for example from Unlisted to Public or Scheduled), to read back a video's status after upload, and to post the channel's own comment on its own video.

The tool does not read, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube user. It does not use YouTube Analytics data.

What the tool stores, and where

  • The Google sign-in token, stored only as a file on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except Google's servers.
  • The IDs, titles and privacy status of the videos it uploaded, stored in local project files on the same computer, so it doesn't upload the same video twice.

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

The stored video IDs are kept as long as the channel owner keeps the project. To remove the tool's access at any time:

  • revoke it at Google Account › Security › Third-party apps with account access, and/or
  • delete the token file on the owner's computer.

Once access is revoked, the tool can no longer access the YouTube account. Videos already on YouTube stay there until the owner deletes them in YouTube Studio.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel owner through the email listed on The Neural Rewiring's YouTube About page.
