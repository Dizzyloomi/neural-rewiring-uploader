Neural Rewiring Uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel The Neural Rewiring (@TheNeuralRewiring). It uses YouTube API Services.

What it does

The Neural Rewiring makes long-form videos about psychology and self-improvement. This tool is the last step of the channel's own production workflow. After the channel owner approves a finished video, the tool:

  • uploads it to the channel as Unlisted (never public),
  • sets its title, description, tags and custom thumbnail,
  • uploads the matching Shorts the same way.

Nothing is ever made public automatically. The owner watches each upload, then publishes or schedules it with a separate, manual action. When a video is published, the tool posts the channel's own pinned comment on it.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It runs on the owner's own computer, is not offered to the public, and signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) to the owner's YouTube account only.

Data

The tool does not collect data about viewers or other users. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what it accesses and stores, and the Terms of Service.
